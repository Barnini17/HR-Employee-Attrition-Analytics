# HR Employee Attrition Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset.

The objective is to understand the factors associated with employee turnover and demonstrate how HR data can be organized, analyzed, and interpreted to support data-driven HR decision-making.

This project is designed as an HR Analytics portfolio project for demonstrating practical skills in:

- HR data analysis
- Employee attrition analysis
- Excel
- PostgreSQL
- SQL
- Data interpretation
- HR reporting and insights

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project aims to answer important HR questions such as:

- What percentage of employees have left the organization?
- Which departments have higher attrition?
- Does overtime relate to employee attrition?
- Does job satisfaction influence attrition?
- How does business travel relate to employee turnover?
- Which job roles experience higher attrition?
- Does income or job level appear to be associated with attrition?
- How do employee tenure and years at the company relate to attrition?
- Which employee characteristics may indicate higher attrition risk?

- Performance Rating
- Relationship Satisfaction
- Stock Option Level
- Total Working Years
- Training Times Last Year
- Work-Life Balance
- Years at Company
- Years in Current Role
- Years Since Last Promotion

The dataset contains **1,470 employee records and 35 columns**.
